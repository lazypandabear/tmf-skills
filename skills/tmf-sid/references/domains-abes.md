# SID Domains and Representative ABEs

Classic GB922 ABE names. Releases add, rename, and regroup ABEs. Verify against the target GB922 release.

| Domain | Representative ABEs |
|---|---|
| Market/Sales | Market Segment, Competitor, Marketing Campaign, Sales Channel, Sales Statistic, Sales Lead/Prospect, Market Strategy & Plan |
| Product | Product Specification, Product Offering, Product, Product Usage, Product Performance, Strategic Product Portfolio Plan, Product Offering Price |
| Customer | Customer, Customer Interaction, Customer Order, Customer Problem, Customer Bill, Applied Customer Billing Rate, Customer Bill Collection, Customer Statistics, Customer SLA |
| Service | Service Specification, Service (CFS, RFS), Service Configuration, Service Performance, Service Usage, Service Trouble, Service Test, Service Strategy & Plan |
| Resource | Resource Specification, Resource (logical, physical), Resource Topology, Resource Configuration, Resource Performance, Resource Trouble, Resource Test, Resource Usage, Resource Strategy & Plan |
| Supplier/Partner | Supplier/Partner, S/P Plan, S/P Interaction, S/P Order, S/P Problem, S/P Bill, S/P Performance |
| Enterprise | Corporate planning, risk, revenue assurance, finance, HR objects |
| Common Business | Party, Party Role, Place/Location, Agreement, Business Interaction, Policy, Usage, Characteristic, Root Business Entity |

## Ownership rule
Each ABE has exactly one system of record. Record it per ABE when mapping applications (see `tmf-functional-framework`).
