# eTOM Structure: Level 0 and Level 1

Classic grouping names. Newer releases restructure by domain. Verify against GB921 for the target release.

## Strategy, Infrastructure & Product (SIP)
Verticals:
- Strategy & Commit
- Infrastructure Lifecycle Management
- Product Lifecycle Management

Horizontals:
- Marketing & Offer Management
- Service Development & Management
- Resource Development & Management
- Supply Chain Development & Management

## Operations (OPS)
Verticals:
- Operations Support & Readiness
- Fulfillment
- Assurance
- Billing & Revenue Management

Horizontals:
- Customer Relationship Management
- Service Management & Operations
- Resource Management & Operations
- Supplier/Partner Relationship Management

## Enterprise Management
- Strategic & Enterprise Planning
- Enterprise Risk Management
- Enterprise Effectiveness Management
- Knowledge & Research Management
- Financial & Asset Management
- Stakeholder & External Relations Management
- Human Resources Management

## Classic grouping to domain mapping
| Classic horizontal | Domain |
|---|---|
| Marketing & Offer Management | Market/Sales, Product |
| Customer Relationship Management | Customer |
| Service Development & Management; Service Management & Operations | Service |
| Resource Development & Management; Resource Management & Operations | Resource |
| Supply Chain Development & Management; Supplier/Partner Relationship Management | Supplier/Partner (Business Partner) |
| Enterprise Management groupings | Enterprise |

## Decomposition levels
| Level | Content |
|---|---|
| L0 | Business activities: SIP, OPS, Enterprise Management |
| L1 | Process groupings: verticals and horizontals |
| L2 | Core processes that combine into end-to-end flows |
| L3 | Tasks and success-path flows |
| L4+ | Steps, error handling, product and geographic variants. Operator-specific. |
