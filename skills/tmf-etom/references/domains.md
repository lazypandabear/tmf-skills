# eTOM Domains and Representative L2 Processes

Classic OPS L2 names. Newer releases rename and regroup. Verify against GB921D for the target release before quoting.

## Market/Sales
Scope: market strategy, segmentation, competitor analysis, campaigns, sales channels, leads and prospects.

## Product
Scope: product strategy and portfolio, offer development and retirement, product performance, product instances delivered to customers.
