# Capability Map Method

Official structure: GB1029C TM Forum Capability Map. The rubric below is a working method, not TM Forum content.

## Maturity rubric
| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | Ad hoc. Manual, person-dependent, no metrics. |
| 2 | Repeatable. Documented, partly tooled, inconsistent data. |
| 3 | Defined. Standard process and system of record, basic KPIs. |
| 4 | Managed. Automated, measured against targets, integrated via APIs. |
| 5 | Optimized. Closed-loop improvement, data-driven, partner-ready. |

## Importance rubric
| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | Commodity. Outsource candidate. |
| 3 | Core. Required to operate. |
| 5 | Differentiating. Drives revenue or strategic position. |
